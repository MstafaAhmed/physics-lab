# 🔬 Physics Lab Experiments & Simulations

Welcome to my **Physics Laboratory and Simulation Portfolio**!

This repository documents physics laboratory experiments, theoretical principles, experimental procedures, data analysis, and computational simulations. It is designed to support students in understanding physics concepts through practical experiments and scientific computing.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Document physics laboratory experiments and experimental procedures.
* Connect theoretical physics with practical measurements.
* Analyze experimental data and visualize physical relationships.
* Develop numerical models and simulations using MATLAB and Python.
* Provide educational resources to help students understand laboratory experiments and prepare their lab reports.

## 📚 Topics

### ⚙️ Classical Mechanics

Experiments involving motion, forces, oscillations, and rotational dynamics.

### ⚡ Electricity and Magnetism

Electrical circuits, resistance, capacitance, magnetic fields, and electromagnetic phenomena.

### 🔭 Optics

Reflection, refraction, lenses, interference, and diffraction.

### 🌡️ Thermodynamics

Heat transfer, thermal properties, and temperature-dependent physical phenomena.

### 💻 Computational Physics

Numerical methods, MATLAB simulations, Python programming, data visualization, and comparison between theoretical and experimental results.

### 🧪 Semiconductor Physics

Semiconductor devices, PN junctions, diode characteristics, and related electrical measurements.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

* MATLAB
* Python
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Microsoft Excel
* Scientific data analysis and visualization

## 📂 What Each Experiment May Include

Depending on the experiment, you may find:

* Objectives and theoretical background
* Experimental apparatus and procedures
* Mathematical equations and sample calculations
* Experimental measurements and data tables
* Graphs and data analysis
* Error analysis and uncertainty calculations
* MATLAB or Python simulation code
* Results, discussion, and conclusions

## 👨‍🔬 About This Project

This is an educational portfolio developed to document physics experiments, improve scientific computing skills, and share learning resources with students and fellow physics enthusiasts.
