Nonlinear pendulum with ode45

g = 9.81; L = 1; b = 0.05;
f = @(t,y) [y(2); -(g/L)*sin(y(1)) - b*y(2)];
[t,y] = ode45(f, [0 12], [pi/6; 0]);
plot(t, rad2deg(y(:,1))); xlabel('t (s)'); ylabel('\theta (deg)');

RC discharge: fit the time constant

t = data(:,1); V = data(:,2);
p = polyfit(t, log(V), 1);          % ln V = ln V0 - t/tau
tau = -1/p(1);
fprintf('tau = %.3f s\n', tau);
