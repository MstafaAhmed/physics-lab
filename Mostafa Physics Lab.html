About

Background, direction and tools.

My academic training is in control and power systems. Over time my interest moved to what sits underneath every circuit: how carriers move in a transistor and how that behaviour can be modeled, simulated and predicted.

I am now building toward research in advanced transistors, 2D materials and machine-learning-assisted TCAD, and I am pursuing graduate study at KFUPM.

The demos below are small, honest models. Each one shows the equation, the simulation and the measured result side by side, which is how I like to check my own understanding.
